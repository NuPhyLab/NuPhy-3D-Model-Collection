# NuPhy 3D Model Collection

Welcome to the official NuPhy 3D Model Collection!

Explore a collection of 3D printable accessories, custom parts, and creative designs for NuPhy keyboards.

From decorative knobs and keycaps to functional accessories, these models are here to help you personalize your setup and bring a little extra creativity to your keyboard.

All models are available for free download for personal, non-commercial use, subject to the applicable permissions and restrictions.

## How to Download & Print

1. Browse the folders and select your preferred model.
2. Open the corresponding folder to view available files and reference images.
3. Download the model files using GitHub's download options.
4. Open the files in compatible 3D modeling or printing software.
5. Check the dimensions, compatibility, and printing requirements before printing.

**Important Notes:**

- Available file formats may include STL, STEP, STP, OBJ, and other related files.
- Some models may require additional components or assembly.
- Printing settings and material requirements may vary by design.
- Compatibility is not guaranteed across all keyboard models or configurations.

## Printing Recommendations

For models with fine details, such as decorative rotary knobs, resin 3D printing is recommended for better detail reproduction.

For functional accessories, please check the dimensions and structural requirements before printing.

Some models may include additional printing recommendations in their respective folders.
